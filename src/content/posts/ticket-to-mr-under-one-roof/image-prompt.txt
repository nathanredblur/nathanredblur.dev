Article: How an AI development tool is built as a layered system that carries a task from ticket to merge request. One orchestrator at the top routes intent and gates the work, single-purpose helpers each own one phase of the cycle, small scripts underneath do the real work and pass structured data upward, and a few side workers absorb noisy investigations in their own space. The piece is about the layers and the clean contract that lets them talk without stepping on each other.
Key elements: a vertical stack of layers connected by arrows flowing top to bottom; an orchestrator hub at the top directing traffic; a row of single-purpose worker blocks each labeled with one job; a base layer of small scripts emitting neat data cards upward; a couple of isolated side workers reading a messy log in their own separate bubble; a task card traveling from a ticket at one end to a merge request at the other.
